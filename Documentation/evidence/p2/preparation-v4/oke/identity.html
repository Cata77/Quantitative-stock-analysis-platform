Frequently Asked Questions

What is the trading symbol for ONEOK, Inc. and on which exchange are its shares traded?

Trading symbol: OKE
Exchange: New York Stock Exchange (NYSE)
CUSIP number: 30609A109 (effective Sept. 11, 2026)

Who is the transfer agent for ONEOK’s common stock?

ONEOK’s transfer agent is EQ.

How do I obtain information about my account?

All account information, as well as assistance with transactions such as stock sales or transfers and address changes, can be handled by contacting our transfer agent, EQ, at 1-866-235-0232 or online.

Can I purchase ONEOK common stock directly from the company? Does ONEOK have a dividend reinvestment plan?

ONEOK's Direct Stock Purchase and Dividend Reinvestment Plan provides new investors and current shareholders a convenient way to purchase ONEOK common stock and to reinvest cash dividends.

How can I obtain a copy of ONEOK’s annual report or quarterly reports to shareholders.

Electronic versions of the annual report and quarterly reports are available online.

Does ONEOK pay a dividend and if so, when does ONEOK pay a dividend on its common stock?

Yes, ONEOK currently pays a quarterly dividend. You may review ONEOK’s dividend history here.

How can I obtain the latest earnings information?

Earnings news releases are available on the Press Releases page. Upcoming earnings release dates are on the Events calendar. You may wish to subscribe to our e-mail notification service to receive news releases and other financial information from ONEOK.

What kind of tax form do I receive?

As a corporation, ONEOK reports shareholder tax information on an annual IRS Form 1099-DIV.
