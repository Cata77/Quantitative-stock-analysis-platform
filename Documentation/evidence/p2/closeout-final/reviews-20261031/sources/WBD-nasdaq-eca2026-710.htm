Equity Corporate Actions Alert #2026 - 710
(UPDATED: Merger closed) Information Regarding the Merger of Warner Bros. Discovery, Inc. (WBD)

Category:

  • Industry Announcement

Markets Impacted:

  • The Nasdaq Stock Market

Contact Information:

  • Nasdaq Corporate Data Operations at +1 877 308 0523
  • Nasdaq Trading Services at +1 212 231 5100

Resources:

  • Press Release

At a special meeting held on April 23, 2026, the shareholders of Warner Bros. Discovery, Inc. (WBD) approved the merger with Paramount Skydance Corporation (PSKY). The merger closed prior to the market open on October 6, 2026. In anticipation of the closing, the stock was halted immediately following the after-hours session at or around 7:50 p.m. on October 5, 2026. The stock will remain halted on the day of closing October 6th and will be suspended effective October 7, 2026. The details are as follows:

Company Name/Issue: Warner Bros. Discovery, Inc. Series A Common Stock
CUSIP#: 934423104
Symbol: WBD
Last Trading Date: October 5, 2026
Marketplace Effective Date for Suspension: October 7, 2026
Merger Consideration: $31.01666668 for each share held.
